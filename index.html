VoiceLeaf

Speak your day. VoiceLeaf writes the diary page.

VoiceLeaf is a voice journal for Android. Tap once, talk for a minute or three about your day, and VoiceLeaf turns what you said into a diary page you can flip through by day, month and year.

Private by design

  • Your speech is turned into text on your phone.
  • Your journal is stored on your phone, encrypted. VoiceLeaf has no server and never receives a copy.
  • Recordings are deleted automatically two days after you make them.
  • Optional Premium backup goes, encrypted, to your own Google Drive — not to us.
