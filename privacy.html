VoiceLeaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

VoiceLeaf is a voice journal. You speak about your day and the app writes a diary page. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: your journal is processed and stored on your phone, and we never receive it.

Your journal stays on your phone

Everything you create in VoiceLeaf — voice recordings, the text of what you said, diary pages, moods, highlights, themes and overviews — is processed and stored on your device, encrypted. VoiceLeaf has no server and no account system. We never receive, see, store or sell your journal.

Speech is turned into text using Android's on-device speech recognition. If the English language pack is not installed, Android's speech service may download it; that download contains none of your content.

Voice recordings are deleted automatically two calendar days after you make them, once their diary page has been written. Your journal is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Information that does use the internet

These services are operated by Google. None of them receives your journal content.

  • Advertising (free version). VoiceLeaf shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app interaction information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Ads are never shown while you record or read an entry. Premium subscribers see no ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Payments. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active; we never receive your payment details.
  • Crash reports and usage counts (optional). If you allow it, VoiceLeaf sends anonymous crash reports (device model, Android version, app version, and technical error details with all messages removed) and a small set of usage counts, such as "an entry was created" or "a backup finished", to Google Firebase. These never include your words, entry dates, moods or any journal content. Advertising ID collection for analytics is disabled. You can turn this off in Settings → Share crash reports and usage counts. In regions that require consent, it is off until you turn it on.
  • Google Drive backup (Premium, optional, off by default). If you turn it on, VoiceLeaf uploads an encrypted copy of your journal (entries, transcripts, moods, highlights — not voice recordings) to a folder named "VoiceLeaf Backups" in your own Google Drive. It is encrypted on your phone before upload, and we cannot read it. VoiceLeaf asks only for access to files it creates, and cannot see anything else in your Drive. We never receive a copy. If your subscription ends or you turn backup off, uploads stop and existing backups stay in your Drive for you to keep or delete.
  • Backup key escrow. To let you restore on a new phone, the key that unlocks your backups may be stored using Android Block Store, which end-to-end encrypts it with your device's screen lock inside your Google account. We cannot access it.

Permissions

  • Microphone — used only while you are recording an entry.
  • Notifications (optional) — for a daily reminder and to tell you when a page is ready. Notifications never contain your journal text.
  • Biometrics / screen lock (optional) — only if you turn on app lock. Authentication is handled by Android.
  • Internet and network state — for ads, subscriptions, optional crash reports and optional Drive backup.

Backup files and exports

You can save an encrypted backup file or a PDF export wherever you choose using Android's file picker. VoiceLeaf does not upload these files. Backups can be opened with this phone, your Google account's device backup, or your recovery code, which is shown once and never stored by the app. If all three are lost, the backup cannot be opened, and we have no way to recover it.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single entry, or all data from Settings → Delete all data, with an option to also delete your VoiceLeaf backups in Google Drive. Uninstalling the app removes all journal data from your phone. You can also delete the "VoiceLeaf Backups" folder from Google Drive yourself at any time. Because we never receive your journal, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

VoiceLeaf is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Moods and overviews in VoiceLeaf are descriptive only. The app does not offer medical, psychological or therapeutic advice.

Changes

If we change how information is handled, we will update this policy and, where it matters, explain the change in the app before anything new happens.
